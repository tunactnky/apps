Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

World Radio ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the World Radio app, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is published by Tunahan Çetinkaya.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personal data such as your name, email address, or phone number.

However, the third-party services used by the app (see sections 3, 4 and 5) may automatically collect certain technical information, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, and app usage data.

2. Location Data

We do not request, collect, or store your location data. You can explore the radio stations on the map without providing any location information.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Radio Browser API (radio-browser.info) to provide radio station information. When you play a radio station, you are connecting directly to the stream provided by that station, which can see your IP address. Third-party stations may have their own privacy policies.

4. Advertising

The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and other technical information to show and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Analytics

We may use anonymous analytics and crash reporting to improve app performance and user experience. This data does not identify you personally.

6. In-App Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play / Apple App Store; we do not receive or store your payment card details.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
